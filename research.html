Research

Aerial robotics, tethered systems, robotic perception and autonomous robots.

Tethered drones

Modeling, simulation, localization and landing assistance for tethered aerial robots. The active project Modelagem e Simulação de um Sistema Umbilical Instrumentado para Drones (FAPEMIG) develops an integrated simulation of a quadrotor connected to an instrumented tether (cable angle, length and tension sensing) and a landing platform that may be fixed or mounted on a ground robot. It builds on my Ph.D. work on tether-based localization and cable-guided landing, with applications in continuous monitoring, inspection and underground mine safety.

Tools: ROS/Gazebo, Project Chrono, CoppeliaSim, C++, Python. Related: JINT 2023, RA-L 2023, Robotics 2025.

VTOL tail-sitter UAV

Project funded by PRPq/UFMG. Design and prototyping of a tail-sitter UAV that switches between vertical and horizontal flight using only its control surfaces, with no extra mechanisms to reconfigure motors or wings. The platform combines a Pixhawk autopilot and a Raspberry Pi companion computer, and the project covers modeling, simulation, CAD, prototyping, avionics integration and flight tests.

Sim-to-real decision making for mobile robots

Project funded by PRPq/UFMG. A Python platform integrating ROS, Gazebo, neural networks and hardware-in-the-loop evaluation for autonomous decisions (control, path planning, navigation, obstacle avoidance). Key questions are how to generate representative synthetic data and how to deploy trained models (e.g., via ONNX) on embedded hardware such as Raspberry Pi and Pixhawk.

Path planning for quadrupedal robots

Postdoctoral work at Instituto Tecnológico Vale (2024–2025) on semantics-aware path planning, where legged robots prefer specific terrains while accounting for safety, energy and time (ICAR 2025, video).

Embedded sensing for UAVs

Earlier work at UFMG and FITec on embedded instrumentation for fixed-wing UAVs: sensor fusion, AHRS design and hardware-in-the-loop evaluation of attitude estimators.
