Rogerio R. Lima

Assistant Professor · Department of Electrical Engineering · Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)

I work on aerial robotics, tethered drones, robotic perception, sensor fusion, applied AI and embedded systems. I received my B.Eng. and M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from UFMG and my Ph.D. in Robotics/Aerospace Engineering from West Virginia University. Before joining UFMG in 2025, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Instituto Tecnológico Vale (ITV), an intern at MathWorks, a lecturer at UniBH, and worked in industry at FITec and Konatus.
